      How scoring works

      Ratings use the Elo system, the same idea chess uses. Everyone starts at 1000. When you win you take points from the other side, and how many depends on how surprising the result was.

      One match

      1. Work out the winner's expected chance of winning from the rating gap:
        expected = 1 / (1 + 10(loser − winner) / 400)
      2. Points moved = 32 × (1 − expected), rounded. 32 is the K-factor: the most one match can move (double for a player in their first 10 games).
      3. The winner gains those points and the loser loses the same amount.
      WinnerLoserExpectedPoints
      1000100050%±16
      1100 (favourite)100064%±12
      1000 (upset)110036%±20
      1200 (big favourite)100076%±8

      New players

      Your first 10 games in a ladder count double for you, so you reach your real level quickly. Your opponent gains or loses the normal amount, so one newcomer can't swing everyone else's rating. The preview before Save shows each player's own points.

      2v2

      Each team's rating is the average of its two players. The points are worked out the same way, then every winner gains them and every loser loses them.

      In Overall, a 2v2 match counts half (an even game moves ±8 instead of ±16). In doubles you share the win with a partner, so a weaker player teamed with a strong one can't climb the main ladder as fast as by winning 1v1. The 1v1 and 2v2 tabs keep their own full-weight ratings, so you can see who is strongest at each format.

      The points shown next to a match in Recent matches are its Overall points.

      Tiers

      Like League of Legends ranked. Your first 5 games are placements; after that you get a tier. The top three placed players hold the apex tiers if they are rated 1025 or more; everyone else is tiered by rating.

      Badges

      • 🔥 Win streak of 3 or more; the number is the streak length. One loss ends it.
      • ⚔️ Giant killer: won against a player (or team) rated 100+ points higher, in the last 7 days.

      Badges follow the tab you're viewing: a 2v2 streak shows on the 2v2 tab, and both formats count toward Overall.

      Good to know

      • The score (e.g. 11–4) is saved for the record but doesn't change points. Only the win counts.
      • During placements (first 5 games) your rating still moves, at double speed; only the tier is hidden until you finish.
      • Ratings are recalculated from the full match history every time, in the order matches were played. Deleting a wrong match corrects everyone's numbers.
      • Hiding a player removes them from the ladder but keeps their matches, so nobody else's rating changes.
